# Abilympics Bangladesh Partners

A premium, accessibility-first partnership and sponsorship landing page for **Abilympics Bangladesh**, created to connect the Bangladesh delegation with corporate partners, sponsors, technology partners, media organizations and supporting organizations ahead of **Abilympics Helsinki 2027**.

The website focuses on Bangladesh's journey from the **10th International Abilympics in Metz 2023** toward the next international stage in Helsinki, Finland.

---

## 🌍 Project Overview

**Abilympics Bangladesh** represents talented professionals with disabilities and their skills on the international stage.

The website is designed as a partnership-focused digital platform rather than a traditional corporate or informational website.

### Primary Goal

The primary objective of the website is to:

- Attract corporate partners and sponsors
- Introduce partnership opportunities
- Showcase Bangladesh's Abilympics journey
- Highlight the achievements of the 2023 delegation
- Introduce participants and stories
- Promote inclusion, skills and professional excellence
- Provide media and social updates
- Make it easy for organizations to contact the Bangladesh team

## ♿ Accessibility

Accessibility is a core requirement of this project.

The website is designed with consideration for people with:

- Low Vision
- Invisible Disabilities
- Visual Disabilities
- Legal Blindness
- Complete Blindness
- Color Blindness
- Deaf-Blindness

The implementation focuses on:

- Semantic HTML
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Accessible forms
- Screen-reader-friendly content
- Meaningful image alt text
- Sufficient color contrast
- Large touch targets
- Accessible carousels
- Accessible modals/lightboxes
- Accessible FAQ interactions
- Reduced-motion support
- Captions-ready video
- Transcript-ready content
- No color-only communication
