## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the confluence of Olympic-tier human excellence, uncompromising institutional dignity, and high-impact corporate sponsorship presentation. Built for the national delegation of Abilympics Bangladesh en route to Helsinki 2027, the visual tone departs from standard non-profit or charitable tropes, establishing an elite athletic-editorial presence that commands authority across government ministries, multinational sponsors, international media, and global vocational competition arenas.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **High-End Editorial Media** with **Architectural Minimalism** and **Technical Glassmorphism**:
- **Monolithic Discipline:** Dense, intentional layouts anchored by rigorous structural grids and unapologetic vertical rhythm.
- **Olympic Dignity:** Expansive negative space framed by monumental typography, elevating vocational mastery (ICT, robotics, craftsmanship, precision engineering) to the tier of world-class athletic disciplines.
- **Bicultural Harmony:** Grounded deep charcoal foundations mirror Nordic modernist restraint, punctuated by Bangladesh's sovereign forest green, balanced by crisp Helsinki glacial ice blues and disciplined pops of radiant crimson.
- **Inclusive Rigor:** Accessibility is treated as an aesthetic benchmark rather than a compliance checklist, ensuring uncompromising clarity, expansive hit targets, and WCAG AAA color contrast ratios across dark and light canvases.

## Colors

The palette operates under a default dark mode engineered for grand stage presentations, immersive video showcases, and luminescent delegate profiles, with paired high-contrast light contexts for white papers, governance documentation, and press releases.

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Forest Green (`#0D5C43` / `#006A4E`):** Represents the national sovereign dignity of Bangladesh. Used for premier badges, delegation insignias, primary focal actions, and formal accreditations.
- **Nordic Glacial Cyan (`#38BDF8` / `#E0F2FE`):** Captures Helsinki's subarctic atmosphere and digital innovation. Serves as interactive telemetry, focal data points, hover states, and navigational indicator highlights.
- **Radiant Crimson (`#F42A41`):** A restrained, laser-focused accent drawing directly from the circle of the national flag. Reserved strictly for live broadcast status, urgent competition milestones, and high-tier calls-to-action.
- **Deep Foundation (`#0B0F14` & `#121820`):** Architectural obsidian slate providing supreme optical contrast against luminous type and glass surfaces.

### Metallics & Medal Tones
- **Podium Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#FDE047`):** Applied exclusively for gold medal prospects, Tier-1 corporate partners, and premier national records.
- **Nordic Bronze (`#B45309` / `#FDBA74`):** Delivers warmth to secondary metrics and institutional backing seals.

### Color Rules & Hierarchy
- Never combine Forest Green and Radiant Crimson as adjacent fills; Crimson must only function as an optical beacon (dots, badges, thin borders) against deep charcoal or pure white.
- Neutral surfaces must retain slate undertones; pure `#000000` is prohibited to maintain rich cinematic depth.
- Body copy on dark surfaces must consistently use `#F8FAFC` or `#E2E8F0` to maintain WCAG AAA compliance.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy projects global competition authority and editorial prestige through three deliberate type choices:

1. **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Display):** Infuses technological precision and competitive sports tension. Its geometric forms evoke high-performance engineering and futuristic Olympic identity.
2. **Inter (Body & System Interfaces):** Provides neutral, hyper-legible reading across long-form candidate profiles, competition rulebooks, and press releases. Ensures zero optical distortion at small scales.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Accents & Metadata):** Dedicated to competitive statistics, countdown clocks, event codes (e.g., `BD-HEL-2027`), scoring rubrics, and category tagging.

### Typographic Principles
- **Monospaced Micro-Headers:** Always precede large display headlines with a mono tag in uppercase tracking (e.g., `JETBRAINS MONO / 11PX / 0.12EM / [ BANGLADESH NATIONAL CONTINGENT ]`).
- **Tabular Numerics:** All scores, timestamps, and competitor counts must render using tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Fluid Line Heights:** Body typography retains generous line heights (1.6x - 1.65x) to offset high-contrast dark-mode reading fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a fluid 12-column grid system paired with strict vertical modules based on an 8pt base unit.

### Breakpoints & Grid Mechanics
- **Desktop Large (≥1440px):** 12-column grid, max content boundary `1360px`, `gutter: 2rem`, outer margins `4rem`.
- **Desktop / Laptop (1024px – 1439px):** 12-column grid, dynamic outer margin `2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, outer margin `2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (≤767px):** 4-column grid, outer margin `1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Structural Rhythm
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature stories and competitor profiles utilize a split 7:5 or 8:4 column rhythm, contrasting large high-impact visuals against tight, monospaced metadata sidecars.
- **Section Pacing:** Primary editorial sections are separated by dramatic breathing room (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` - `8rem` on desktop displays) to instill institutional grandeur.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation eschews fuzzy skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of **Architectural Glassmorphism**, layered charcoal tiers, and hairline light-refracting borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F14`):** The foundational substrate of the application.
- **Level 1 Surface (`#121820`):** Structural panels, editorial sidebars, and fixed utility navigation bars. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 Interactive Glass (`rgba(18, 24, 32, 0.75)` with `16px backdrop-blur`): Floating cards, delegate profiles, and active dialog overlays. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)` on Nordic focal elements or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` on standard blocks.
- **Level 3 Modal / Flightdeck (`#18202A`):** Critical interactive modals, filter shelves, and live telemetry panels. Accompanied by a controlled ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Lighting & Borders
- **Nordic Rim Light:** Active cards feature an inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Medal Glows:** Podium tier cards project a soft, atmospheric colored radiance:
  - Gold: `0 0 40px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`
  - Ice Blue: `0 0 35px -8px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural curvature level (`roundedness: 1`). Soft, controlled corner treatments convey institutional solidity and technical mastery without appearing overly play-centric or frivolous.

- **Micro Components (Chips, Tags, Inputs):** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, precise, and instrument-like.
- **Medium Structural Elements (Cards, Panels, Media Tiles):** `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers subtle, deliberate frame containment.
- **Floating Modals & Hero Canvas Banners:** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Special Pill Overlays (Status Badges, Live Beacon Pills):** Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for circular indicators such as `● LIVE HELSINKI` or category count markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign:** Solid `#0D5C43` with crisp white text (`Inter / 14px / SemiBold`). On hover, transitions to `#0A4834` accompanied by a refined Nordic Cyan outline (`1px solid #38BDF8`).
- **Nordic Secondary:** Translucent ice glass (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)`), framed in `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`, text in `#38BDF8`.
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, `JetBrains Mono / 12px / Uppercase`, paired with an animated micro arrow glyph `→`.

### Competitor & Delegate Cards
- Framed in Level 2 Glass with top-right monospaced classification stamps (`CAT-ICT-01`).
- Delegate portrait utilizes a duotone/monochrome treatment with high contrast, bursting into full natural color on hover alongside a subtle gold or cyan rim border.
- Integrated medal milestone badges pinned to bottom corner with `JetBrains Mono` tabular scoring.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Compact height (`24px`), built with `JetBrains Mono / 11px / Tracking: 0.08em`.
- **Helsinki Countdown Chip:** Glacial translucent background with a pulsing `#38BDF8` status dot.
- **Official Contingent Seal:** Forest green fill with `#FFFFFF` text and a micro embossed crest.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#121820`, bottom border accented `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On focus: dynamic transition to Nordic Ice Cyan (`#38BDF8`) with an inner glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp `2px` corners, solid charcoal interior with high-contrast `#38BDF8` tick markers and `#0D5C43` selection states. Fully keyboard accessible with custom `2px` cyan focus rings.

### Editorial Metric Display (Sponsorship & Impact)
- Split statistics block displaying large figures in `Space Grotesk / Display-Hero` with paired label sidecars in `JetBrains Mono / Label-Caps`.
- Subtly intersected by hairline Nordic Cyan separators (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`).
