Where Ability Takes Center Stage.

Abilympics is the premier quadrennial world skills championship for persons with disabilities. Certified vocational specialists, engineers, programmers, designers, and artisans gather to demonstrate world-class capabilities across high-demand technical trades.

Known internationally as the “Olympics of Abilities”, Abilympics redefines workplace equity by proving that mastery, discipline, and grit overcome any barrier. Competitions are benchmarked directly against rigorous WorldSkills industry standards.

Our Journey Started in Metz.

In March 2023, the red and green flag of Bangladesh flew proudly at the 10th International Abilympics in Metz, France. Organized through CSID and United Action, an inspired delegation achieved unprecedented glory.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers regarding Abilympics, Helsinki 2027, and corporate CSR partnerships.

The Abilympics (Olympics of Abilities) is the premier quadrennial world championship for vocational skills where professionals with disabilities demonstrate world-class capabilities across digital, artisanal, and engineering vocations.
Abilympics Helsinki 2027 is the 11th International Abilympics competition, scheduled for 10–13 May 2027 at the Messukeskus Helsinki Expo Centre in Finland, hosting over 43 technical competition trades.
Represented by a 10-member official delegation with 7 active contestants organized through CSID and United Action, Team Bangladesh made history by capturing two international medals on the global stage.
Candidates undergo transparent regional and national skill assessments conducted by vocational experts, followed by multi-month rigorous bootcamps benchmarked to WorldSkills and IAF competition criteria.
Organizations can sponsor across structured tiers (Official, Corporate, Technology, Media, and Supporting Partner) or support specific items like travel logistics, accessible technology workstations, and coaching grants.
Partners receive validated social impact metrics, high-visibility corporate diversity credits, verified carbon/CSR documentation, and direct participation in national workplace inclusion forums.
You can request the prospectus instantly via the partnership portal at partners.epyra.agency or complete the contact inquiry below for dedicated briefing material.
Follow our verified social broadcasting handles on Facebook and YouTube, bookmark the live website ticker, and subscribe via our secretariat form for quarterly delegation bulletins.
